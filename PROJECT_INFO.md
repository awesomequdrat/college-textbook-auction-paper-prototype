## My Documented Contribution
**Task 2 - Bid on an Item**

The project report explicitly identifies Qudrat Siyal as the designer of the bidding task. The flow includes:

- keyword-based textbook search;
- filtering search results;
- viewing a selected book in detail;
- reviewing title, author, description, current bid, and auction end time;
- entering a bid amount;
- placing a bid;
- confirming or canceling the action.
